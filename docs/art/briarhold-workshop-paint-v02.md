# Briarhold workshop painting v02

[Open the painted workshop in the walkable review](../../assets/reference/concept/environments/chapter-5-interior-masters-v01/briarhold-interior-review-v01.html?map=workshop&paint=1). The square painting follows the approved station layout. The west window sits over the wax stove, and the press, stamp chest and low tray shelf occupy the west wall. The cooling rack screens the east-side pocket. The north and east stairs and the wash-yard door remain separate.

The built-in image generation tool used the annotated workshop layout for placement and the painted hub for materials. Version 2 clears the floor beside the press for the worker and supervisor and gives the stair lamp a short wall mount. One 2048 × 2048 normalized painting covers both guide states. The approved v02 painting is registered in Chapter 5.
