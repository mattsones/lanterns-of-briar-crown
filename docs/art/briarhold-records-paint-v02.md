# Briarhold records room painting v06

The approved v06 painting is registered in Chapter 5. The records room uses an oak floor, a stone stair and one oil lamp on the writing desk. The longer left turn ends in the widened pocket south of the spare desk. The matching spare desk runs north–south by the west wall, with its drawer and tucked chair facing east and its oil lamp unlit. A second chair is tucked under the working desk. Two low crates adjoin the south end of the spare desk and a separate supply crate sits beside the lit desk. The west tally recess opens into the room. The desk drawer stays closed, with the charter and other evidence handled separately by the game.

The built-in image generation tool used the records layout for geometry and the painted hub for style. Version 2 opened the tally recess. Versions 3–5 widened the entry, matched and rotated the spare desk, added chairs, and deepened the south pocket. The square 1254 × 1254 native source is preserved, and the review uses a 2048 × 2048 derivative normalized without a crop.
