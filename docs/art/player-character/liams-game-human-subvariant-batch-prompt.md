# Liam's Game - Human Sub-Variant Batch Prompt

Use this prompt with the selected European Human male and female hero images attached as visual references.

```text
Create six full-body Human hero variants for Liam's Game, a warm hand-painted storybook fantasy adventure RPG.

Use the two attached selected Human hero images as the direct style, pose, scale, body-proportion, clothing-complexity, rendering, and composition references. Treat them as the current European Human male and European Human female versions. Your task is to create matching Human sub-variants with different broad regional ancestry reads while preserving the same game-asset language.

Generate these six separate images:

1. African Human male hero
2. African Human female hero
3. South American Human male hero
4. South American Human female hero
5. Asian Human male hero
6. Asian Human female hero

Each character should be a young adult village adventurer at the beginning of the journey: brave, sincere, adaptable, capable but not epic. They should all read clearly as ordinary humans, with no magical ancestry traits, no elf ears, no elemental features, no glowing marks, no animal traits, and no monster features.

Keep all six as travel-ready hero base art. Use practical rustic village-travel clothes, sturdy boots, a short weathered cloak or scarf, and a small belt pouch. Keep them in the same general clothing tier as the references: humble, handmade, road-ready, and not ornate. You may adjust colors, textiles, hair, facial features, skin tones, and small personal details to make each regional ancestry read distinct, but avoid turning them into ceremonial costumes, national costumes, stereotypes, royalty, warriors, or historical reenactment outfits.

For the female variants: make them clearly adult young women with feminine adult silhouettes, modest natural curves, visible waist and hip shape, and practical storybook proportions. They should read feminine rather than boxy, and adult rather than childlike, while staying modest, road-ready, and family-friendly.

For the male variants: make them clearly young adult men with grounded, approachable faces and practical adventurer builds. They should feel capable, warm, and village-born rather than grim, muscular, royal, or epic.

Composition requirements:
- Full-body character illustration, vertical 2:3 composition.
- Prefer 1024 x 1536 px or larger for each image.
- Transparent background preferred.
- If transparency is not possible, use a plain light neutral background that can be removed later.
- The character should be centered and fully visible from head to boots.
- Keep the face and upper body crop-safe for portrait reuse.
- Keep the head/face large and clear enough to crop into small circular map tokens.
- Use a natural standing travel pose, not a rigid model pose and not an action-combat pose.
- No large shoulder satchel, no backpack, no visible weapons, no heavy armor.

Style requirements:
Storybook fantasy character art with a hand-drawn, hand-painted feel, subtle painterly texture, soft brushwork, gentle ink-like definition, and a warm illustrated-book atmosphere. The art should feel handmade rather than slick or overtly digital. Use a middle-ground character style: expressive and warm, like storybook villagers and young adventurers, but not overly cartoony or exaggerated. Cozy fantasy tone, sincere and adventurous, family-friendly, with gentle whimsy. Use natural fantasy materials, practical village-travel clothing, soft parchment warmth in the palette, and a clear readable silhouette.

Batch consistency requirements:
- The six images should feel like they belong to the same character selection set as the attached European Human references.
- Keep the same approximate camera distance, full-body scale, and vertical placement across all six.
- Keep the art direction consistent across male and female variants.
- Do not make any variant look more heroic, rich, armored, magical, or important than the others.
- Do not change the Human ancestry into another playable ancestry.
- Do not include text, labels, nameplates, scenery, props floating separately, UI frames, or decorative backgrounds.

Use fantasy-world labels in the game UI and asset registry:

Avoid:
photorealism, 3D-rendered look, glossy digital game art, anime style, harsh comic-book ink, horror tone, grimdark mood, over-saturated neon colors, modern clothing, sci-fi elements, excessive clutter, exaggerated caricature proportions, chibi proportions, childlike proportions, child-coded faces, pin-up styling, sexualized costume, aggressive grim warrior look, franchise resemblance, national flags, modern symbols, text, labels, background scenery, large shoulder satchel, backpack, visible weapons, heavy armor, ornate royal costume, ceremonial costume, stereotype costume, and floor shadows that extend beyond the character layer.
```
