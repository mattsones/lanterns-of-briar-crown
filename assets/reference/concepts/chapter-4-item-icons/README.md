## Shared art direction

Stylized storybook-fantasy inventory icon, matching the existing Liam's Game item art: a single isolated object, three-quarter view where appropriate, compact readable silhouette, dark iron and rootwood materials, restrained antique-brass fittings, warm lantern highlights, painterly but smoothly resolved surfaces, and no border, label, scenery, cast shadow, or text. The Gatewright equipment should feel like ingenious tunnel-working gear rather than conventional military equipment.

Item-specific direction:

- Folded Map Scrap: worn folded parchment fragment with overlapping folds and sparse hand-drawn route marks; no lantern-cache symbol or legible writing.
- Gatewright Hookblade: short hooked iron tool-weapon with rootwood grip and tunnel-maintenance practicality.
- Gatewright Passage Pike: visibly long, narrow-shafted passage weapon with a compact piercing head; revised after the first pass to avoid reading as a dagger.
- Gatewright Counterweight Maul: balanced, engineered tunnel maul whose counterweight construction is apparent in silhouette.
- Ironroot Ribplate: layered iron-and-rootwood torso armor with ribbed structure and plausible mobility.
- Low-Arch Roothelm: low-profile rootwood-and-iron helm made to clear cramped tunnel ceilings.
- Old Waykeeper Helm: older patinated helm with distinctive flared listening guards and a visibly different historical construction.
- Lio's Courier Knot: recognizable blue courier cord tied in a deliberate compact knot; no bead, seal, tag, or other unsupported ornament.
