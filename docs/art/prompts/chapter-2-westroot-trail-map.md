# Chapter 2 Westroot Trail Map Prompt

Use this prompt for the painted gameplay-map background for Chapter 2.

```text
Create a hand-painted storybook fantasy gameplay map for "Liam's Game," Chapter 2: The Westroot Trail. The image should be a top-down / slightly isometric painted map background designed for tile-based movement, not a poster or cinematic landscape.

Scene: a hidden forest road west of Bramblecross, where the honest Lantern Road has been covered by false signs and overgrown briars. The map should include these readable gameplay locations arranged as distinct zones:
- Old Westward Cut: a narrow old road entrance with trimmed roots and a subtle Lantern Road marker.
- Shelter Nook: a safe mossy resting hollow with a small lean-to, warm stones, and travel bundles.
- False Notice: a suspicious posted sign nailed too aggressively to a tree, with copied wax/seal imagery.
- Three-Sign Hollow: a small clearing with three different road signs; one should feel honest and quiet, one loud and official, one misleading and messy.
- Roadwatcher encounter area: a darker bend with thorn-scraps, hanging sign-cloth, and watchful briar shapes.
- First Westroot Gate: an old root-and-stone threshold with three door-like symbols, including one "no-handle" door mark that feels honest and hidden.

Style: warm children's fantasy storybook painting, watercolor/gouache texture, cozy but mysterious, clear silhouettes, readable paths, no modern objects, no text labels baked into the art. Use earthy greens, gold lantern light, bark browns, cool moss shadows, and small hints of red-briar danger. Keep the center and path areas visually clean enough for game tokens and interactable labels.

Composition requirements:
- 16:9 landscape, suitable for a React game map background.
- Leave slight safe margins around all edges.
- Make paths and landmarks align visually to a tile/node-based map.
- Avoid UI, characters, words, captions, logos, watermarks, heavy fog, extreme darkness, or photorealism.
- The map should feel playable: the player can visually understand where to walk, where to stop, and what places are important.

Output as a high-resolution PNG. Prefer crisp details and layered-looking brushwork, but keep contrast balanced so game overlays remain legible.
```

Negative prompt:

```text
No photorealism, no 3D render, no anime, no horror, no grimdark palette, no modern roads, no readable text labels, no logo, no watermark, no UI panel, no character closeups, no heavy fog hiding landmarks, no clutter that obscures paths.
```
