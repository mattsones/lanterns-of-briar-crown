# Briarhold hub painting v03

The guide-closed and guide-open paintings are ready for review under the existing walkable hub layout. Version 2 gives the guard-bay lantern a bolted mount on its north wall and the lower workshop-stair lantern a curved iron bracket from the west stair wall. Version 3 shifts the lower stair lantern closer to the west wall and uses a short hook patterned after the receiving-entry fixture. The compact bracket is present in both lighting states.

[Open the painted hub in the walkable review](../../assets/reference/concept/environments/chapter-5-interior-masters-v01/briarhold-interior-review-v01.html?map=hub&paint=1). Use **Amber guide open** to switch the lighting and **Painted hub** to compare against the technical map. The review retains the movement, actor and clearance overlays. For the clean painting, turn off actors and use **Clean composition**.

The built-in image generation tool used the hub composition master for geometry and the accepted rescue painting for materials. Native images are 1448 × 1086. The review uses 2048 × 1536 WebP derivatives normalized without a crop.
