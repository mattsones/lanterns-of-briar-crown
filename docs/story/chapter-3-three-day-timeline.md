# Chapter 3 Three-Day Timeline

Last updated: 2026-07-24

Status: Working chronology paired with `chapter-3-story-truth.md`. Day 3 is the playable day of Chapter 3; the earlier events are backstory the player reconstructs.

## Day 1 — Three Nights Before The Party

| Time | Event | What Westroot believes | What is actually happening |
|---|---|---|---|
| Late afternoon | A Willow-marked freight convoy reaches the First Gate during second watch. It presents a valid-looking outside seal, Westroot account mark, and correct watch timing. | A rare but authorized essential transfer has arrived. | The Willow seal and inside account have been copied. A Briar cargo operative is confirming the movement. |
| At the Transfer Checkpoint | The gatekeepers wash and inspect one crate, its handlers, and an unnamed hooded courier, then hold them at the account rail while they compare the outside papers with Westroot's mark. | The transfer looks valid but must complete the post-Witherdeath sanitation and ordinary threshold check. | The courier is Lio, restrained and unable to give his name. He scratches his hook-tailed mark near the gate while the account is checked. |
| Rootbread service | A Mossback child helping Lume brings the checkpoint's standard bread-and-water tray to everyone waiting for admission. | The Rootbread Promise applies before trust is settled; refusing the tray would be unusual. | The handlers accept the routine to avoid attention. Lio eats, ties blue thread around the cup in his hooked knot, and returns it on the tray. |
| Early evening | The checkpoint admits the transfer. The crate is unloaded into Cargo Siding and Lio is moved into the guarded transfer corridor. | The freight and courier are following separate assigned routes. | The crate is operational cover. Lio is being moved as a prisoner beside it, not inside it. |
| Night | Lio is taken through the Lower West Gate into the Underway. | The unnamed courier transfer has left Westroot under the admitted account. | The Briar Crown is moving Lio toward Briarhold Waystation. His pouch and transfer tag remain in the crate. |

## Day 2 — The Hold Begins

| Time | Event | What Westroot believes | What is actually happening |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Quill and Bramwell compare the freight ledger with the physical Willow seal. The sender details do not agree. | The shipment may be counterfeit, so its account and related signals require review. | The review is the first institutional action that threatens to expose the Briar route. |
| Midday | Bramwell places the crate under hold. He closes the Witness Stone walk and listening marks pending Split Hall review. | A limited lawful pause will prevent the disputed account from authorizing more movement. | The hold secures the marked bay but also prevents the outer-shelter water call from receiving a witnessed mark. |
| Second water bell | Westroot misses its first scheduled call to the six people at the outer cistern shelter. | The delay is an uncomfortable but temporary cost of the cargo review. | Isolation has begun harming people unconnected to the crate. |
| Night | The child keeps the returned cup on the checkpoint shelf because its blue knot looks deliberate, though they do not know whose sign it is. | A traveler thanked the checkpoint in an unfamiliar courier fashion. | Lio has already been moved west. The cup is personal evidence that he was fed there. |

## Day 3 — The Playable Chapter

| Time | Event | What the player must understand |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Liam's party opens the First Gate through the ancient phrase-operated mechanism. No scheduled account, inside confirmation, or Gatehand key is used. | Westroot is alarmed by an **unscheduled kind of opening**, not by proof that the door has never moved before. |
| During the gate commotion | The hidden Briar cargo operative moves the held crate behind the rear stacks, brushes the wheel tracks, and resets the siding lock. | The party's arrival does not bring the crate into Westroot; it gives the operative a distraction in which to conceal evidence already there. |
| Morning | Bramwell grants a limited welcome at the same Transfer Checkpoint after the party completes its visible sanitation routine. Rootmarket, Quill, Lume, Noma, and the Mossgarden establish Westroot's hidden operation and internal disagreement. | Westroot ended routine surface contact after Witherdeath about sixty years ago, including its close Bramblecross partnership, but maintained rare sanitized transfers and essential outer services. |
| Optional Rootbread return | Lume remembers that her young helper served the unnamed courier from the Willow convoy. The party returns to the checkpoint; the child shows Mara the blue-thread cup and recounts the transfer. Liam and Mara restock the tray. | The Rootbread Promise reached Lio, he traveled beside the crate, and the player continues the promise through a concrete checkpoint action. |
| Second water bell | Westroot misses the outer shelter's second scheduled call because the Witness Stone listening marks remain under hold. | Bramwell's limited precaution now has a concrete cost to six named people. |
| Late morning | A runner discovers the empty hold bay and rings the Hold Bell. Bramwell orders the First Gate, side passages, and listening signals closed. | This is a new total emergency lockdown, broader than the earlier Willow hold. |
| Midday | Split Hall hears the cargo risk, the private watch timing, and the missed outer-shelter calls. The party volunteers to investigate. | Both caution and communication protect real people; neither can operate without named responsibility. |
| Early afternoon | Bramwell and Noma lift the Witness Stone hold together. Westroot sends the overdue water call and publicly authorizes the Cargo Siding investigation. | The stones reopen civic work and one investigation. They do not yet reopen Westroot generally to Bramblecross. |
| Afternoon | The party follows the physical tracks, finds the relocated crate, and confronts the Briar operative. | Lio was treated as a freight transfer but was never inside the crate. The crate contains the tools and records of the operation. |
| Late afternoon | Split Hall receives the evidence and adopts the new compact. | Isolation prevented Westroot from checking the Willow claim. Westroot will remain guarded but make Bramblecross its first restored surface contact, using named people and records to verify future warnings and cargo. |
| Dusk | Quill carries the first written warning to Enna through the guarded First Gate. Noma reveals the westward Brindle record in the Mossgarden. | The reconnection has physically begun, and Lio is alive farther west in the Underway. |

## Causal Spine

The Briar Crown's forged trust admits the convoy. Westroot's fear delays open comparison. The limited hold blocks useful communication but fails to secure the evidence. The player's unscheduled arrival exposes the weakness; the hidden operative's attempt to conceal the crate triggers the full lockdown. Witnessed investigation proves that secrecy alone did not protect Westroot, leading to controlled reconnection rather than unrestricted opening.
