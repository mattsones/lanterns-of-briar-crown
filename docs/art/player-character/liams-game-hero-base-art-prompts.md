# Liam's Game - Hero Base Art Prompts

These prompts are for basic travel-ready hero illustrations, not paper-doll rigs or equipment overlays.

Use each prompt as a standalone copy/paste prompt. The repeated style and negative instructions are included in every prompt on purpose.

## Human Male Hero

```text
Create a full-body Human male hero for Liam's Game, a warm hand-painted storybook fantasy adventure RPG.

He is a young adult village adventurer at the beginning of his journey: brave, sincere, adaptable, capable but not epic. He has a warm approachable face, grounded human features, practical rustic travel clothes, sturdy boots, a short weathered cloak or scarf, and a small belt pouch. Keep him clearly human, with no magical ancestry traits.

The image should be a full-body character illustration for display in a game character screen. Use a natural standing travel pose, not a rigid model pose and not an action-combat pose. Make the face and upper body crop-safe for possible portrait reuse, and keep the full-body silhouette clean enough for possible map-token cropping. Use a vertical 2:3 composition, preferably around 1024 x 1536 px or larger. Transparent background preferred; if transparency is not possible, use a plain light neutral background that can be removed later.

Style: storybook fantasy character art with a hand-drawn, hand-painted feel, subtle painterly texture, soft brushwork, gentle ink-like definition, and a warm illustrated-book atmosphere. The art should feel handmade rather than slick or overtly digital. Use a middle-ground character style: expressive and warm, like storybook villagers and young adventurers, but not overly cartoony or exaggerated. Cozy fantasy tone, sincere and adventurous, family-friendly, with gentle whimsy. Use natural fantasy materials, practical village-travel clothing, soft parchment warmth in the palette, and a clear readable silhouette.

Avoid: photorealism, 3D-rendered look, glossy digital game art, anime style, harsh comic-book ink, horror tone, grimdark mood, over-saturated neon colors, modern clothing, sci-fi elements, excessive clutter, exaggerated caricature proportions, chibi proportions, pin-up styling, aggressive grim warrior look, franchise resemblance, text, labels, background scenery, large shoulder satchel, backpack, visible weapons, heavy armor, ornate royal costume, and floor shadows that extend beyond the character layer.
```
