# Chapter 3 Player-Knowledge Contract

Last updated: 2026-07-27

Status: Implemented acceptance contract paired with `chapter-3-story-truth.md`. The canonical script, runtime, map interaction, quest copy, art, and automated Chapter 3 playtest now carry these required facts. A fresh human comprehension playthrough remains the final release-candidate signoff.

## Clarity Rules

1. Deliver the physical fact before using a metaphor about it.
2. Use **First Gate**, **Transfer Checkpoint**, **Witness Stone hold**, **listening marks**, **Cargo Siding**, and **Lower West Gate** consistently. Do not call all of them simply *the gate* or *the road*.
3. State the scope whenever something opens or closes: people, cargo, signals, or one investigation.
4. Optional scenes may deepen emotion or provide corroboration; they may not carry facts required to understand the main plot.
5. A scene may leave one purposeful question open only after its immediate action is clear.

## Transfer Checkpoint Interaction Contract

The optional objective becomes **Follow the Rootbread Clue**. Lume's lead activates the child at the First Gate landing; it does not complete the thread from Rootmarket.

At the checkpoint, the player can ask two short questions in either order:

- **What did the courier look like?** The child describes a hooded young traveler with handlers standing too close and a Willow crate waiting at the same account rail.
- **Why did the handlers let you feed him?** The child explains that every waiting traveler receives the checkpoint tray. Refusing it would have made the false transfer conspicuous.

Both questions remain available until asked. The child then produces the returned cup, Mara identifies Lio's hooked blue knot, and the player chooses **Restock the tray for the next traveler**. That physical action grants the Rootbread Charm and completes with **The Rootbread Promise reached Lio**.

After completion, the child marker disappears. Inspecting the First Gate may review the restocked tray and the cup entered into Westroot's witness record, but it grants no repeat reward.

## Optional-Content Boundary

The Rootbread Promise should reward exploration with personal proof that Lio received care and remained resourceful. It requires a deliberate return interaction at the First Gate's Transfer Checkpoint; Lume's dialogue alone cannot complete it. A player who skips it must still learn in Cargo Siding that Lio traveled beside the convoy and was not inside the crate. Likewise, optional Split Hall testimony may add names and emotional weight, but the required path must communicate the two missed water calls and the correct private timing.

## End-Of-Chapter Comprehension Check

A player who finishes Chapter 3 should be able to answer these questions without guessing:

1. Why does Bramblecross think Westroot is a legend even though Westroot still handles rare transfers?
2. How did the Briar Crown make its convoy look legitimate?
3. Was Lio inside the Willow crate?
4. How did Lio return his blue string at the Transfer Checkpoint?
5. Why was the party's opening of the First Gate alarming?
6. What did Bramwell and Noma reopen at the Witness Stones?
7. What new relationship did Split Hall authorize with Bramblecross?

If a playtester cannot answer any of these, revise the earliest required scene assigned to deliver that fact. Do not add a late explanatory monologue.
